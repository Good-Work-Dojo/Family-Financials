## What you may do without asking

- Run, copy and share unmodified copies of the program under its own name.
- Say what your work is built on: "based on Family Financials", "a fork of Family Financials", "works with Family
  Financials".
- Name the project in articles, talks, reviews, tutorials and comparisons.
- Keep the attribution line that `NOTICE` requires in the About screen and the sign-in page.

## What needs a new name

A version whose code you have changed is your own, so it needs a name and a logo of its own:

- Rename it: its title, the sign-in page's heading, the web manifest and the images' descriptions say your name, not
  "Family Financials".
- Replace the logo with one of your own that cannot be mistaken for it.
- Do not suggest that the project made, checked or supports your version.

Keeping the line "Based on Family Financials, © 2026 …" from `NOTICE` in the Appropriate Legal Notices your version's
interfaces display (in this program the About screen and the sign-in page) is how a renamed version credits the
original; it does not make the version "Family Financials".
